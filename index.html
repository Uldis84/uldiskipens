Podcast Short · Edit House · DACH

Rainer Schanz im Tim Gabel Podcast

Short-form edit for the Tim Gabel Podcast featuring Rainer Schanz, former personal bodyguard of Angela Merkel. Precise caption timing that reinforces the spoken rhythm, plus clean audio-flow cuts that pull the viewer through the interview without losing a single natural moment.

Precision captions every word sits on the beat, not beside it. Captions as an active editing tool, not an afterthought.
Audio-flow editing cuts follow speech rhythm and emphasis, not a fixed frequency. Sounds natural, but is tightly structured.
Retention-optimised pacing opens on the strongest line, no dead intro, clean loop point at the end.

Frequently asked.

Everything worth knowing before we work together.

What type of content do you specialise in?+
Mainly long-form YouTube: food, travel, lifestyle. But as you can see with the shorts, I can adapt to fast-paced humorous content too. Whatever the format, I approach it the same way: make it hold attention.
What's your turnaround time?+
For a typical 10 to 20 minute YouTube video: first cut within 5 to 7 days. Rush delivery is possible, just reach out and we'll figure it out.
Which software do you use?+
Adobe Premiere Pro for editing, After Effects for motion graphics. I deliver in any format, optimised for YouTube or raw ProRes.
How many revisions are included?+
2 rounds of revisions as standard. Anything extra is a minor additional cost.
What do you need from me to get started?+
Raw footage, reference videos, a rough outline of the story, and your music preferences. That's it. I'll handle the rest.
