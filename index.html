Storytelling Edit

Narrative-driven long-form

A video built around emotional arc — from cold open hook to satisfying close, with every section earning the viewer's attention.

Cold-open hook structure — drops the viewer into the most compelling moment before context, forcing commitment in the first 8 seconds.
Dynamic pacing system — alternates between fast-cut energy and held moments, reducing drop-off at the mid-video plateau.
Music as a narrative tool — tracks are cut to emotion, not the other way around; swells and drops reinforce story beats.

Storytelling Edit

Character-led documentary style

The edit follows the emotional journey of the subject, using intercutting and sound design to create tension and release across the runtime.

Custom paper-texture overlays — add tactile warmth and visual identity that separates this from generic travel content.
Intercutting for tension — parallel storylines cut together at key moments to manufacture suspense without manufactured drama.
SFX layering & sound design — ambient audio beds, foley accents, and score transitions create immersion beyond the talking head.

Frequently asked.

Everything you need to know before working together.

What type of content do you specialise in?+
I specialise in long-form YouTube content, primarily food, travel, and lifestyle. Videos that need strong narrative structure, good pacing, and retention-focused editing.
What's your turnaround time?+
For a typical 10–20 minute YouTube video I deliver a first cut within 5–7 days. Rush delivery is available, just reach out.
Which software do you use?+
Adobe Premiere Pro for editing, After Effects for motion graphics and titles. I deliver in any format — optimised for YouTube or raw ProRes.
How many revisions are included?+
2 rounds of revisions included as standard. Everything else are minor extra costs.
What do you need from me to get started?+
Raw footage, reference videos, a brief outline of the story, and your music preferences. That's it. I handle the rest.
